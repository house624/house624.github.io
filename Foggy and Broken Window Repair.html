Fog or moisture between window panes?

Persistent haze or condensation trapped between double-pane glass usually signals that the insulated glass unit’s edge seal has failed. Outside air and moisture can enter the space between panes, leaving cloudiness that ordinary window cleaning cannot remove. A failed seal can also reduce the insulated glass unit’s insulating performance.

The right solution depends on the condition of the glass and frame. When the frame remains sound, replacing the insulated glass unit may restore a clear view without replacing the entire window assembly. We can inspect the window and explain the available repair options.

Cracked or broken window glass

A cracked or shattered pane can leave a home exposed to weather and create a safety concern. We replace residential window glass and assess whether the existing frame can be kept. The replacement glass is selected to fit the opening and the window?s requirements.

If glass is broken, keep people and pets away from the area. Do not handle loose shards. Contact us to discuss the window and arrange an estimate.

Frequently asked questions

Can you fix fog between double-pane glass?

Fog trapped between panes usually points to a failed insulated-glass seal. Cleaning the outside surfaces will not remove moisture trapped inside. Replacing the insulated glass unit is often an option when the frame is in suitable condition.

Do I need to replace the whole window?

Not always. If the existing frame is sound and compatible with replacement glass, glass-only replacement may be possible. We assess the window before recommending an approach.

What affects the estimate?

Price depends on dimensions, glass type and specifications, access, and the condition of the frame. Contact us with the window details for an estimate.

What should I do if a window pane breaks?

Keep people and pets clear, and do not handle loose glass. Contact us to discuss safe next steps and replacement options.
