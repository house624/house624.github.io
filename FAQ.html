Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, honest answers about residential window repair, foggy glass replacement, and weatherization in the DFW area.

Q: What causes home windows to get foggy or cloudy?

Foggy windows are caused by a phenomenon called "failed thermal seals." Modern residential windows use double-pane insulated glass units (IGUs) sealed tightly together with a dead airspace in between. North Texas weather puts massive stress on these seals through hot-and-cold cycles (solar pumping). Once the seal ruptures, outside humidity creeps into the airspace, creating condensation, dampness, or a white haze that you cannot wipe away.

Q: Do I have to replace the whole window frame if only the glass is bad?

Absolutely not! Many large window sales companies will try to convince you to tear out your entire vinyl or aluminum window frame system, which can cost thousands of dollars. At Picture Perfect Window Solutions, we specialize in high-efficiency glass-only replacement. We leave your perfectly good existing frames completely untouched and simply install a brand-new, sealed double-pane glass unit inside them. This saves you significant money, creates zero structural mess, and restores your energy efficiency.

Q: Can I use your online calculator if I don't know the exact window measurements?

Yes! Our homepage tool is designed to give you a quick, rough budget estimate. You can simply measure the visible glass area from the inside of your home using a standard tape measure to get your rough dimensions. Don't worry about being off by a fraction of an inch—our crew will always come out to your house to take exact, professional, laser-verified measurements before cutting any replacement glass pane.

Q: What is window weatherizing and do I need it?

Weatherization involves replacing old, brittle, or torn perimeter seals, bulb seals, and pile weatherstripping directly inside your window frame architecture. Over time, Texas heat shrinks these seals, allowing outside drafts, wind, moisture, and fine dust to blow directly into your home. Weatherizing stops drafts instantly, locks your expensive air-conditioned HVAC air inside, and drops your monthly energy bills significantly.
