Custom Glass Shower Doors & Enclosures

Picture Perfect Window Solutions designs and installs custom framed, semi-frameless, and frameless glass showers to fit your bathroom, style, and needs.

Request a Free Estimate Call or Text (817) 647-0229

Choose the shower style that fits your bathroom

Every bathroom is different. We help you choose an enclosure style based on your layout, glass preferences, hardware, and budget, then measure and plan the installation for your space.

Framed glass showers

Framed enclosures use a metal frame around the glass panels. They offer a defined, traditional look and can suit a variety of shower openings and bathroom designs.

Semi-frameless glass showers

Semi-frameless enclosures combine framed sections with exposed glass edges. This option balances a lighter, more open appearance with the support and structure of framing where it is needed.

Frameless glass showers

Frameless enclosures use heavy glass with minimal visible framing. They create clean sightlines and an open feel, with hardware and configuration selected for your bathroom.

Custom measurements and installation

A good fit starts with careful measurements of the shower opening, walls, curb, and surrounding surfaces. We discuss door swing or sliding configuration, glass layout, and hardware choices before the glass is specified.

Our team plans the installation around the bathroom?s actual conditions and checks clearances so the enclosure can open and close properly. Contact us to discuss your project and arrange an estimate.

Frequently asked questions

Do you install framed, semi-frameless, and frameless showers?

Yes. We offer all three styles and can help you compare them for your bathroom layout and preferences.

How do I choose the right enclosure?

Consider the opening, desired look, hardware, and budget. We can review the space and explain the trade-offs among framed, semi-frameless, and frameless options.

Can you make a shower enclosure for my bathroom dimensions?

We measure the opening and plan the glass and hardware around the space. Contact us to discuss the bathroom and request an estimate.

What affects the cost of a custom glass shower?

Cost depends on the opening size, enclosure style, glass configuration, hardware, and installation conditions. We provide an estimate based on your project details.

Where do you install custom showers?

We serve the DFW and surrounding areas, including Fort Worth, Dallas, Burleson, Crowley, Cleburne, Alvarado, Mansfield, Midlothian, and Godley.
